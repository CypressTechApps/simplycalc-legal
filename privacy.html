Privacy Policy

Simply Calculator for Android · Last updated 10 October 2026

The short version

Your calculations happen on your phone. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and we operate no server that anything is sent to. The only part of the app that deals with anything outside your phone is Google Play's purchase system, for the one-time unlock.

We do not receive, see, or store anything you type into the app.

What the app stores, and where

Your calculation history — the running tape of expressions and their results, and any names you give to pinned lines — is written to storage that belongs to this app on your own device. So are your settings, such as degrees or radians, your theme choice, and which converter categories are switched on.

  • Nothing you calculate is transmitted anywhere.
  • The unit converter and every tool (tip and split, percent, loan, dates, fractions, programmer, fuel and unit price, statistics) work entirely offline. Conversion factors are built into the app; there is no lookup, no rate fetch, and no network request behind any result.
  • You can clear your history in the app at any time. Pinned lines stay until you unpin them.
  • Sharing your history is something only you start: the app hands the text to the Android share sheet and you choose where it goes.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. The app also opts out of Android's cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer, so no copy is made on your behalf. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore it for you.

What is collected, and by whom

Simply Calculator collects nothing, and it contains no advertising or analytics software. The one third party involved is Google Play, and only if you buy the one-time unlock.

DataCollected byPurpose
Purchase transactionGoogle PlayProcessing and restoring your purchase

That transaction is between you and Google and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your payment details; the app is only told whether the unlock is owned.

Permissions

  • Google Play billing — to offer and restore the one-time unlock.
  • Internet / network state — declared by Google Play's billing library, which the app includes for that purchase. Simply Calculator's own code makes no network request, and nothing you calculate is ever sent.
  • Vibration — for the optional key-press feedback, which you can switch off in Settings.

Simply Calculator requests no other permissions. It has no access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, or notifications, and it does no work in the background.

Children

Simply Calculator is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Clear your calculation history in the app at any time.
  • Uninstall to erase everything on the device.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request — but if you have a question, please write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects how data is handled, note it in the app's release notes.
