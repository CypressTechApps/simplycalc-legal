Privacy Policy

Simply Calculator for Android · Last updated 19 August 2026

The short version

Your calculations happen on your phone. There are no accounts, and we operate no server that anything is sent to. The app uses the internet for two things only: the advertising banner on the free tier, and Google Play purchases.

We do not receive, see, or store anything you type into the app.

What the app stores, and where

Your calculation history — the running tape of expressions and their results — is written to storage that belongs to this app on your own device. So are your settings, such as degrees or radians and your theme choice.

  • Nothing you calculate is transmitted anywhere.
  • The unit converter works entirely offline. Its conversion factors are built into the app; there is no lookup, no rate fetch, and no network request behind any conversion.
  • You can clear your history in the app at any time.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore it for you.

What is collected, and by whom

Simply Calculator itself collects nothing. On the free tier, however, the app displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob, and that SDK collects data. If you buy the one-time premium upgrade, the advertising SDK is never started at all and none of the following applies.

DataCollected byPurpose
Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)Google AdMobAdvertising, measurement, fraud prevention
Approximate location, derived from IP addressGoogle AdMobServing regionally appropriate ads
App interaction and diagnostic dataGoogle AdMobAd delivery and performance
Purchase transactionGoogle PlayProcessing and restoring your purchase

None of this includes what you calculate. Google's handling of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app presents a consent form on first launch, and you can change that choice later in the app's settings.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — advertising and Google Play purchases only. The app makes no network request of its own.

Simply Calculator requests no other permissions. It has no access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, or notifications, and it does no work in the background.

Children

Simply Calculator is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Clear your calculation history in the app at any time.
  • Uninstall to erase everything on the device.
  • Buy premium to stop the advertising SDK from running.
  • Reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request — but if you have a question, please write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects how data is handled, note it in the app's release notes.
